The pipeline

From a deed on paper
to an NFT you can borrow against.

Every asset passes through a deterministic four-step pipeline. Legal ownership is established off-chain, cryptographic ownership on-chain — and the two are permanently linked by hash.

01

Verification

Licensed appraisers, custodians and title registries submit attestations. Documents are hashed and pinned to IPFS with a Chainlink oracle feed.

02

Legal wrapper

An SPV (Cayman / Swiss / Delaware) takes legal title. Token holders hold beneficial ownership — enforceable in court, not just in code.

03

Mint ERC-721

The SPV calls mintAsset(). Metadata, valuation, jurisdiction and custodian are written immutably into the token.

04

Fractionalize

Split the NFT into 1,000–1,000,000 ERC-20 shares, list on the AssetChain AMM, and use the position as collateral in our lending vaults.

What exactly is a "real-world asset" on AssetChain?+
Any tangible or contractual asset with a provable owner and a cash-flow or appraisal value: allocated gold, real estate, solar and wind infrastructure, farmland, equipment leases, invoices, and fine art. Each one is held by a bankruptcy-remote SPV, so token holders hold beneficial ownership rather than a mere claim against the platform.
How is the $RWA presale priced, and when do tokens unlock?+
Stage 3 tokens are priced at $0.042 with a 5% bonus on purchases above 2 ETH. At TGE, 25% of your allocation unlocks immediately and the remaining 75% streams linearly over six months. Tokens are claimable directly from the AssetChainICO contract — no vesting portal, no custody risk.
Is this legal? What about securities regulation?+
Asset NFTs are issued under Reg S / Reg D exemptions for non-US participants and accredited US investors, with a transfer-restriction layer enforced on-chain via ERC-3643 compatible hooks. Every SPV is registered in a recognized jurisdiction and audited annually. This page is not an offer to sell securities in any jurisdiction where such an offer is unlawful.
Can I really borrow against an NFT of a building?+
Yes. Our lending vaults accept tokenized RWAs as collateral at a maximum 150% collateralization ratio, using a Chainlink price feed plus a quarterly third-party appraisal. Loans are issued in $RWA-pegged stable assets or ETH, and liquidations are handled by a Dutch auction that preserves the underlying SPV.
How do I receive the actual yield from the assets?+
Rent, coupons and lease payments flow into the SPV's bank account, are converted to stablecoins, and pushed on-chain by the custodian. From there, the distribution contract splits the funds pro-rata to NFT holders automatically — you claim whenever you want, and there is no minimum.
What happens if the protocol shuts down?+
Assets live in the SPV, not in the protocol. If AssetChain's front-end or AMM ever shut down, token holders retain a direct, legally enforceable beneficial interest in the SPV and can redeem through the custodian. Contract ownership is held by a 48-hour timelock controlled by a multi-sig of six independent signers.
